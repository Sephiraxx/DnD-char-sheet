Fireball

  • Ember bead arcs in, then a 2-frame light/dark flash held through a ~160 ms hitstop.
  • Staged: flash → billowing fireball → black smoke takes over → embers settle.
  • Dust shockwave on the ground, scorch decal that steps out. Big random shake.

Magic Missile

  • Darts fan out from the hand and accelerate into the target (they never miss).
  • Each hit gets a short stop and a tiny vertical-only shake, so 3 darts read as 3 thumps.

Eldritch Blast

  • Beam fires in ~1 frame, thick, then thins and breaks into dashes.
  • Rings ride the beam; the hit is a jagged star around a void that implodes. Knockback per beam.

Thorn Whip

  • Whip wave travels down a thorned vine; a crack on contact, then the vine goes taut.
  • The target is yanked toward the caster (the spell's 10 ft pull) and the vine retracts.
